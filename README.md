## Overview

River discharge is critical to human societies and ecosystem health, yet monitoring it at large scales remains costly and labor-intensive. NASA’s Surface Water and Ocean Topography (SWOT) mission provides satellite-derived river discharge observations with unprecedented coverage and accuracy.

The SWOT River Discharge Explorer is an interactive web application for exploring river discharge across North America. Built on NASA PO.DAAC’s Hydrocron service and the SWORD river network, the application enables users to discover, visualize, and download river discharge time series.

Explore the live application: [SWOT River Discharge Explorer](https://punwath.github.io/swot-river-discharge-explorer/)

By making SWOT observations easier to access and interpret, the platform helps researchers and water resource professionals identify discharge trends and better understand how North American rivers respond to environmental change.
